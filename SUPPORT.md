# Support policy

InkDOS Hub is a personal project, made in spare time. There is no promise of support, fixes or quick answers.

- **Tested on:** iPhone and iPad (Safari and XeOS) and a Windows laptop (Chromium). Other devices may work, but are not tested.
- **Questions and bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/InkDOSHub/inkdoshub.github.io/issues/new/choose).
- **Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF:** the InkDOS apps, ArtCraft and BentoPDF are maintained here as copies; report bugs in this repository.
- **Security:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
