InkSoC runs Windows programs (.exe, 64-bit and 32-bit) in this tab with its own engine: no Wine install, no Linux, nothing sent to a server.

Programs with windows appear on the desktop; console programs open in a console window. Each program gets a button in the bar below: tap it to bring it to the front or hide it, ✕ stops it.

"Open .exe…" takes the .exe together with its DLLs and data files; files the program creates can be downloaded from its console window at the end. Several cores need the hub.inkdos.workers.dev address.

The figure in the bar is the speed of the last program, in billions of x86 instructions per second.
