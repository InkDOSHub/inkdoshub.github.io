InkDOS Hub Games
i

Plays your own game files on this device with EmulatorJS (RetroArch cores): PlayStation (PCSX ReARMed), Super Nintendo (Snes9x), Nintendo 64 (Mupen64Plus), NES (FCEUmm) and Game Boy Advance (mGBA). No games or BIOS come with the Hub: use copies of games you own. PlayStation: .chd, .pbp, .cue with its .bin files (select them together), .bin, .iso or .m3u. The PlayStation BIOS is optional (scph5501.bin or similar improves compatibility); pick it once and it stays on this device. Saves stay in this browser. On iPad and iPhone touch buttons appear on the screen; Bluetooth controllers also work.
