Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows computer. Safari (iPad, iPhone, Mac) does not let websites capture other windows’ sound.

Screen recording (iPad)

i
  1. Open Control Center and touch and hold Screen Recording. Turn the microphone on if you also want your voice mixed in.
  2. Start the recording, watch what you want, then stop it. It goes to Photos.
  3. Tap “Choose the recording” below: only the sound is kept (.m4a, in the list below). The video is not stored here; delete it from Photos if you no longer need it.
