The PC creates its own Wi-Fi network for the phone: the fastest option, without internet and without a router. For a PC with Wi-Fi, without administrator rights.

  1. Open "InkDOSHub-Transferir.exe". If SmartScreen warns, click "More info" › "Run anyway" (the app is not signed).
  2. The control panel opens in the PC browser and creates the network.
  3. Scan the QR codes with the phone camera and send or receive files. The speed shows in real time.

Windows 10 or 11. Keep the app open during the transfer. Received files go to Downloads › InkDOS Hub Transferir. Use Diagnostics to measure the signal, delay and real speed. Source code: windows/transferir (MIT).

Fast file transfer between the phone (iPhone or Android) and the PC. Nothing is installed on the phone.

Both devices on the same network (the PC can be on a cable). Nothing to install. The pairing uses the free public PeerJS server; the files go directly between the two devices and are not uploaded. Public networks may isolate the devices.
