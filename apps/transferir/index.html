InkDOS Hub Transfer (test)

Transfer Test

Fast file transfer between the phone (iPhone or Android) and a Windows PC, without internet, without a server and without administrator rights. Nothing is installed on the phone.

Download for Windows (.exe)

  1. Open "InkDOSHub-Transferir.exe". If SmartScreen warns, click "More info" › "Run anyway" (the app is not signed).
  2. The control panel opens in the PC browser. Choose how to connect: Direct Wi-Fi or Through the router.
  3. Scan the QR codes with the phone camera and send or receive files. The speed shows in real time.
Direct Wi-FiThe PC creates its own Wi-Fi network for the phone. Fastest: the file goes in a single hop through the air. Needs a PC with Wi-Fi.
Through the routerUses the network the PC already has, even by cable. The phone stays on the local Wi-Fi. Public networks may isolate the devices.

Windows 10 or 11. Keep the app open during the transfer. Received files go to Downloads › InkDOS Hub Transferir. Use Diagnostics to measure the signal, delay and real speed. Source code: windows/transferir (MIT).
