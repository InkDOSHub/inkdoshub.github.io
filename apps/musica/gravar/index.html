This browser cannot record the computer’s sound

Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows computer. Safari (iPad, iPhone, Mac) does not let websites capture other windows’ sound.

Records only the sound of the computer, in a small file

  1. Open what you want to record in another tab or window.
  2. Tap Record. In the window that appears, choose the tab with the sound (keep “Also share tab audio” on) or Entire screen (turn on “Also share system audio”).
  3. Keep this tab open. Tap Stop, or “Stop sharing”, when you are done.

About 22 MB per hour (Opus, .webm). Nothing is sent anywhere: the file stays on this computer.

With a folder, the file is written while it records and appears there when you stop. Without one, it goes to Downloads and to the Music list.
